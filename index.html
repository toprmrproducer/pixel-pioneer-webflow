Everything You Need to Know

Quick answers about courses, placement and getting started
Courses
Placement
Joining
I am a fresher with no IT background. Can I join?

Yes. The first weeks build the foundation, and your counselling call places you on the right track. Many students start from zero.

Which course should I pick?

We map it to your background on a free call. Popular paths: Cloud Engineer, DevOps Engineer and System Administrator.

Are classes hands-on?

Yes. Every topic is followed by a lab on real servers and cloud accounts, and you finish with projects you can demo.

Will I get a certificate?

Yes, a Pixel Pioneer completion certificate, plus preparation for industry exams such as AWS Cloud Practitioner.

Do you guarantee a job?

No honest institute can. We guarantee the work that gets people hired: skills, projects, resume, mock interviews and active placement support.

What does placement support include?

Resume and LinkedIn rebuild, mock interviews, curated openings, referrals and help through the final round and the offer.

Can I join only for mock interviews?

Yes. Mock interviews and placement support are open to learners who trained elsewhere.

How much does it cost?

Fees depend on the track and batch. Book a free call and we will walk you through everything, including payment options, before you commit.

Where are classes held?

At our centre in Karve Nagar, Pune, with live labs on site. Ask about online or hybrid options on the call.

How do I register?

Use the form on this page or call us. A counsellor will contact you within one working day.
